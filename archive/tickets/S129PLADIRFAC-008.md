# S129PLADIRFAC-008: Maintenance — place dirtiness decay and basin natural refill

**Status**: COMPLETED
**Priority**: MEDIUM
**Effort**: Small
**Engine Changes**: Yes — extends `item_decay_system` maintenance pass with two new per-tick mutations
**Deps**: archive/tickets/S129PLADIRFAC-001.md

## Problem

Without per-tick decay, `PlaceDirtiness.value` only ever increases — places can never recover from accumulated wilderness relief, breaking FND-11's positive-feedback dampener mandate. Without basin natural refill, `WashBasinState.clean_water_units` is consumed once and never regenerates, so the supply chain (well → basin → wash) collapses after a few uses. This ticket adds both maintenance behaviors as new steps inside the existing `item_decay_system` pass — they share the per-tick maintenance window and avoid introducing a new SystemFn (per spec's "SystemFn Integration" note).

## What to Change

### 1. Add `place_dirtiness_decay` step in `item_decay.rs`

Add `collect_place_dirtiness_decay()` and `apply_place_dirtiness_decay()` to `item_decay.rs`. The collect step visits `query_place_dirtiness()`, skips zero-valued places, computes `value.saturating_sub(decay_per_tick)`, and skips no-op updates. The apply step commits the new component through a hidden maintenance `WorldTxn` with no domain-specific event tag.

Wire both into `item_decay_system`'s body after existing waste decay and harvest-trace pruning.

### 2. Add `wash_basin_refill` step in `item_decay.rs`

Add `collect_wash_basin_refill_targets()`, `next_wash_basin_refill()`, and `apply_wash_basin_refill()`. The collect step records underfull basins that have a place and nonzero `refill_per_tick`; the apply step rereads the basin and first co-located Water `ResourceSource` at that moment, computes the bounded transfer, then commits both component updates through one hidden maintenance `WorldTxn`.

Source lookup uses `query_resource_source()` plus `resource_place()` so the live `ResourceSource` component contract is honored for both place entities and co-located facilities.

### 3. Wire into `item_decay_system`

In `item_decay_system` body, after the existing decay/pruning calls, invoke the two new collect/apply paths in deterministic order: place dirtiness decay first, basin refill second.

## Out of Scope

- `LatrineFullness` decay — explicitly out of scope per spec D9 ("`LatrineFullness` does *not* decay automatically without a maintenance action — full latrines stay full, providing the negative feedback for the system").

- Decay/refill cadence variation per scenario — `decay_per_tick` and `refill_per_tick` are per-component fields authored at scenario-spawn time (ticket 011); no global override knob.
- AI candidate generation/ranking using these decayed/refilled values (deferred to tickets 009 and 010).
