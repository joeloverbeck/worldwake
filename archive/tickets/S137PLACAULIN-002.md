## Problem

S137 deliverable D11 adds two per-agent budget fields to `CognitiveProfile`:
- `repair_budget_fraction: Permille` — bounds the repair search at `repair_budget_fraction × max_node_expansions`.
- `causal_links_per_step_cap: u8` — caps `PlanGuard.causal_links` length (consumed by ticket 004's field addition).

Without these fields, downstream tickets (004 reads the cap; 006 reads the budget fraction) cannot enforce the bounded-repair invariant from S137 Design Goal 3.
